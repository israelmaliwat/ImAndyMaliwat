Capstone Project · Team of 4

SmartHub

Generative AI and AR Tile Visualization Virtual Kiosk with Inventory Management System

Client: Tile Depot Discount Center, Paco, Manila

A web system that replaces the store's manual Excel inventory and helps customers picture tiles in their own space. It has three parts that share one Firebase backend with near real-time updates:

  • Admin Portal (owner): stock levels, sales, deliveries, event logs, low-stock alerts, and sales-forecast suggestions for restocking and promos. Secured with email, password, and a one-time code from Google Authenticator (TOTP).
  • Virtual Kiosk (staff and walk-in customers): browse the tile catalog, add to cart, check out, staff confirms payment, stock is deducted automatically, and an order summary is emailed to the customer.
  • Public link: a limited online catalog with the same visualization tools.

Tile preview modes

  • Generative AI mode: uses the Google Gemini API to replace the floor or wall in a photo of the customer's room with the selected tile.
  • Live AR mode: shows the tile on a real floor or wall through the device camera (WebXR + Three.js on Android, 8th Wall on iOS).
